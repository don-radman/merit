A quiet industrial confidence.

LAYOUT & MOTION
Calm is a design decision.
Use an 8 px spacing rhythm, precise alignment and generous margins. Financial information
should be easier to scan than the surrounding brand imagery.
6-column editorial grid
Spacing
4 px for small internal adjustments; 8 / 16 / 24 /
32 / 48 / 64 px for layout. UI radius: 6 px
controls, 10 px cards. Avoid nested card
borders.
Hierarchy
One dominant message per composition. One
primary action per view. Use hairline dividers,
not heavy boxes. Keep money and statuses
aligned.
Motion
120 ms hover, 220 ms state transition, 420 ms
row arrival. Ease: cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1). Move
new rows at most 4 px.
Reduced motion
Remove movement and staggering when
requested. No pulse loops, bouncing figures or
celebratory effects around money.

18
CLAIMS & STORYTELLING
Credibility is a brand asset.
The brand can describe a future ambition. Product claims must describe the specific release
being shown.
Safe working description
Merit helps fund managers and advisers track introductions and manage placement-fee payouts.
When showing the demo
Label testnet, fictional fund and illustrative data clearly. Keep stub and live demonstrations distinct.
Before claiming automation
Confirm the actual payout path, signer, scheduling and failure handling. A simulated button is not a
production settlement service.
Before claiming confidentiality
Verify the deployed proof system and each visible data field. Rate privacy does not mean all activity is
private.
Before claiming assurance
Do not imply regulatory approval, guaranteed returns, universal compliance or completed audits
without release-specific evidence.
